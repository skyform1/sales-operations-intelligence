# Executive Decision Case | Sales Operations Intelligence

**Context:** Fictional global B2B sales organization, synthetic Q4 2026 pipeline. **Decision:** How should leadership allocate a hypothetical $350,000 discretionary pilot budget across contracting, seller enablement, pipeline quality, territory planning, and approval tooling?

## Decision framing

The objective is to choose a limited, measurable set of pilots, not to maximize speculative revenue projections. The simulation enumerates affordable initiative combinations and makes the chosen downside/upside weighting explicit. Each proposal identifies cost, owner, execution risk, and assumed benefit band.

## Executive recommendation protocol

1. Inspect forecast downside/base/upside without claiming empirical confidence intervals.
2. Compare feasible portfolios against the $350,000 budget; review the ranking under conservative and optimistic assumptions.
3. Check for double counting, dependencies, and whether projected benefits represent bookings, efficiency savings, or forecast accuracy. The prototype's hypothetical bands are not validated causal uplift.
4. Seek Finance and Legal validation, name accountable owners, and define baseline metrics and stop/go thresholds before committing budget.
5. Pilot with measurement checkpoints at 30, 60 and 90 days. Escalate to leadership only after evaluating actual outcomes.

## Strategic trade-offs

- **Contracting:** Faster approvals may improve deal timing but are not equivalent to net-new revenue.
- **Forecast hygiene:** More accurate forecasting may improve planning even with zero incremental bookings.
- **Seller enablement:** Adoption and seller outcomes require observation; avoid equating activity with productivity.
- **Territory changes:** Fairness and coverage may improve without increasing total bookings.
- **Automation:** Tooling has higher integration, policy, and execution risk.

## Interview narrative

This independent portfolio exercise demonstrates translating ambiguous operational priorities into a decision model, presenting alternatives, quantifying assumptions, and designing human approval gates. It is not presented as real client delivery, measured revenue uplift, or prior sales operations employment.
